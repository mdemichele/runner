# Runner Support

Runner is a simple running tracker for iPhone. It times your runs, measures distance and pace using GPS, and keeps a history of your completed runs on your device.

## Getting started

1. Open Runner and tap **Start** on the Run tab.
2. When asked, allow location access so Runner can measure your distance.
3. Tap **Pause** to take a break and **Resume** to continue. Tap **Stop** (or **Save** while paused) to finish and save your run.
4. See your past runs in the **History** tab. Swipe left on a run to delete it.

## Common questions

**Distance shows 0.00 mi.**
Runner needs location access to measure distance. Go to **Settings › Privacy & Security › Location Services › Runner** and choose **While Using the App**. Distance can also stay at zero for the first few moments of a run while your phone gets a good GPS signal, so it helps to start outdoors with a clear view of the sky.

**Does Runner keep tracking when my screen is locked?**
Yes. During a run, Runner keeps measuring distance while your phone is locked. You'll see a blue location indicator at the top of the screen while a run is in progress. Tracking stops when you pause or finish the run.

**Can I use kilometers?**
Not yet. Runner currently shows distance in miles and pace in minutes per mile.

**Where is my data stored?**
Only on your iPhone. Runner has no accounts and doesn't connect to the internet. See the [privacy policy](privacy) for details.

**How do I delete my data?**
Swipe left on a run in the History tab to delete it. Deleting the app removes all of its data.
