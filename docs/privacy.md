# Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** November 13, 2026

Runner is a running tracker for iPhone. This policy explains what information the app uses and what happens to it. The short version: **Runner does not collect, share, or sell any of your data. Everything stays on your device.**

## Information the app uses

### Location

Runner uses your device's location to measure the distance of your runs. Location is used only while a run is in progress, including while your phone is locked during a run. It is not used when you are not running.

Your location and route are never stored. Runner only uses location readings to calculate distance, then discards them.

You can turn off location access at any time in **Settings › Privacy & Security › Location Services**. Without it, Runner can still time your runs but can't measure distance or pace.

### Run history

When you finish a run, Runner saves its date, duration, and distance on your device so you can see your run history. This information:

- is stored only on your device,
- is never sent to us or anyone else,
- may be included in your device's iCloud or computer backups, which Apple manages under its own privacy policy.

You can delete a run by swiping on it in the History tab. Deleting the app removes all of its data from your device.

## What we don't do

- We don't collect any personal information.
- We don't use analytics, advertising, or tracking tools.
- We don't include any third-party code that collects data.
- We don't have servers or accounts. Runner doesn't connect to the internet.

## Children's privacy

Runner doesn't collect information from anyone, including children under 13.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
